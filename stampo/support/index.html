Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Stampo, go to Profile → Settings, and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and cloud-synced profile and stamp records tied to it.

Can I use Stampo without an account?

Yes. You can continue as a guest. Guest data stays on your device and may be lost if you uninstall the app or change devices. Creating an account enables cloud sync for your profile and stamp records.

How do I export my passport?

From the passport view, tap the export/share icon to generate an image of your stamped pages, which you can save to Photos or share.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Email us at stampo.support@gmail.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot. Let us know your device model and app version.
