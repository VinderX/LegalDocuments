Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Stampo ("we", "us", "our") provides a virtual travel passport app that lets you record and collect stamps for countries you have visited. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what choices you have.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address
  • Auth identifiers created through Supabase Auth

Travel data

  • Countries you have marked as visited
  • Visit dates you enter
  • Notes you write for each stamp (up to 140 characters)
  • Stamp styles and customization choices

Technical and usage data

  • Basic app, device, and diagnostic information needed to operate the service
  • Timestamps and identifiers tied to account actions and saved records

2. How we use your information

  • create and maintain your account
  • store and display your travel records, stamps, and notes
  • deliver the core passport and map experience
  • generate your passport and map export images
  • support account deletion, troubleshooting, and service security

3. Who we share information with

We share data only as needed to operate the app:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and backend functions

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or security of users or the service.

4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account remains active.

Your travel records, stamps, and notes are retained while your account is active and until you delete them or delete your account.

We may retain limited records when reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or security.

5. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account from inside the app.

When you delete your account, we delete Stampo-owned account data associated with that user, subject to any limited retention needed for legal or security reasons.

6. Your choices

  • edit or delete individual stamp records in the app
  • delete your account from inside the app

If you no longer want us to process your data, you may stop using the service and delete your account.

7. Children

Stampo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live, including where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
