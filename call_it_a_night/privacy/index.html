Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Call It a Night ("we", "us", "our") provides a bedtime reflection and wind-down app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what choices you have.

1. What we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Account information

  • Email address
  • Auth identifiers created through Supabase Auth

Profile and settings

  • Name
  • Gender
  • Birthday
  • Preferred language
  • Reminder time
  • Notification preference
  • Sleep reasons you select or write

Reflection and history data

  • Scripted or AI chat inputs you submit
  • Completed conversation history, when history/memory is enabled for your plan
  • Short session summaries and emotion summaries generated from completed sessions

Subscription and billing state

  • Whether you have an active Plus subscription or trial
  • Purchase status and entitlement state from RevenueCat and Apple

Technical and usage data

  • Basic app, device, and diagnostic information needed to operate the service
  • Timestamps and identifiers tied to account actions, purchases, reminders, and saved sessions

2. How we use your information

  • create and maintain your account
  • save your profile, reasons, settings, and reminder preferences
  • deliver the core bedtime reflection experience
  • generate AI responses, summaries, and memory features when available on your plan
  • show your saved history and summaries
  • determine access to Free, trial, and Plus features
  • process subscription state and purchase restoration
  • support account deletion, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service security

3. AI features

When AI features are used, the content you submit in the chat experience may be sent to our AI provider to generate a response and session summary.

At the current stage of the product:

  • Free uses scripted chat by default
  • Plus may use AI-based multi-turn chat and memory/history features
  • completed AI sessions may retain full conversation history and a short summary when the feature is available for your plan

We do not use your chat content to advertise to you.

4. Who we share information with

We share data only as needed to operate the app:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and backend functions
  • OpenAI: AI response and summary generation
  • RevenueCat: subscription and entitlement management
  • Apple: App Store purchases, subscription billing, and related account flows

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or security of users or the service.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account remains active, unless a shorter retention period is required for a specific category of data.

At the current stage:

  • account, profile, settings, reasons, and subscription mirror data are kept while your account is active
  • history, transcripts, and summaries are kept while the related feature is available to your account and until you delete your account or we otherwise remove the data

We may retain limited records when reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, billing reconciliation, or security.

6. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account from inside the app.

When you delete your account, we delete Call It a Night-owned account data associated with that user, subject to any limited retention needed for legal, security, or billing reconciliation reasons.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Apple subscriptions must be managed separately through your Apple account.

7. Your choices

  • update profile and reminder settings in the app
  • choose whether to enable reminders
  • view certain saved history when your plan includes that feature
  • delete your account from inside the app

If you no longer want us to process your data, you may stop using the service and delete your account.

8. Children

Call It a Night is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live, including where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and may provide additional notice where appropriate.

12. Contact

If you have privacy questions or requests, contact:

privacy@callitanight.app
